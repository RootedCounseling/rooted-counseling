# A few spots to look at, Dad

The site is live. Everything on it is accurate — but there are 8 short passages
that are meant to sound like *you* personally (how a session with you feels, your
take on faith, what someone's going through in each specialty) that we wrote as
placeholders just to keep things moving. They're not bad, but they're not really
your words yet.

**Nothing needs to happen right away.** Read through them whenever you get a
chance. For each one: if it sounds like something you'd actually say, leave it —
if not, send a replacement sentence or two (a text or email is fine) and it'll
get swapped in, or edit it yourself the way EDITING.md describes.

## About page

**How I work** (currently):
> Sessions with me are unhurried and honest. We go at your pace, and nothing you say will shock me.

**On faith** (currently):
> Faith is a meaningful part of my own story, and if it's part of yours, we can bring it into our work together. If it isn't, that changes nothing about the care you'll receive.

## Specialty pages — the opening line on each one

**Relational Betrayal Trauma:**
> You found out, and now you can't stop replaying it. The person you trusted most became the person who hurt you most, and nobody around you understands what that does to a mind and body.

**Sex Addiction Recovery:**
> You've promised yourself it was the last time more times than you can count. The shame is constant, the secrecy is exhausting, and you're afraid of what honesty would cost.

**Marriage & Relationship Challenges:**
> The same fight keeps happening, or worse — the fights stopped and the silence moved in. You remember being close and don't know how to get back there.

**Anxiety & Depression:**
> Getting through the day takes everything you have, and everyone keeps telling you to just relax or cheer up — as if you hadn't thought of that.

**Healing from Past Trauma:**
> It happened years ago, but it doesn't feel finished. It shows up in your relationships, your reactions, your sense of what you deserve — and you've never told anyone the whole story.

**Preparing Youth to Launch into Adulthood:**
> Your son or daughter is standing at the edge of adulthood, and you're not sure they're ready — or maybe you're the one standing there yourself, unsure which direction to go.

## How to change one

Easiest way: just send the new wording to whoever's helping you with the site and
it gets typed in. If you want to do it yourself, [docs/EDITING.md](EDITING.md)
walks through it — it's a normal web form, not code.
